Dear Zyy ❤️


Happy Valentine’s Day, My Zyy.
I know these past few weeks have been difficult for the both of us, and I can feel that things have changed between us. I understand that I’ve probably hurt you alot, especially with that one mistake that made you so sad. I can't undo what I've done zyy.

But I wish I could take it all back, but I can’t. All I can do is keep saying I'm sorry, over and over, hoping na mapatawad ako nung puso mo. Siguro sinasabi na sayo ng iba na you should leave me, but they don’t understand our story, they don’t see the love that we’ve built or how much we value each other. Tayo lang may alam non.

If these past na mga pangyayari have changed how you see me, I hope na these memories na makikita mo sa baba reminds you of how deeply I care for you. Gusto ko malaman mo kung gano ko kagusto magbago and to grow for you. I’m sorry for letting you down, for messing up again and again.

And even if someday everything changes, gusto ko malaman mo na

Kahit ano man ang mangyari, you will always be my favorite person. Kahit na palagi tayong nag-aaway, it’s always worth it kasi it's with you, whether it’s good or bad. I can’t imagine my life without you, and I’m so thankful na andito ka pa rin. Thank you again for your Valentine's gift. Honestly, I feel like I don’t deserve it, but it means everything to me.

So, you may ask, why did I pick this song? I chose "14" by Silent Sanctuary because it speaks so much of how I feel. "Even though we always fight, you're the only one I want to be with. I’ll give everything, and I’ll be with you anywhere, no matter where." Despite everything, you're the only one I want by my side. You are my peace, my safe place, and the one I want to share my world with.

Happy Valentine’s Day, My Zyy.
I love you more than words can say. I love you endlessly, Zyy. ❤️
